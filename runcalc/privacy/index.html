Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

1. Introduction

RunCalc is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data RunCalc accesses, how it is used, and what we will never do with it.

2. Data We Collect

RunCalc reads HealthKit workout data, including workouts and GPS routes, on your device to provide its core features. RunCalc stores saved routes, route analysis, and goals in its local app database. The revised 1.0 release syncs goal definitions and reusable route templates through your private iCloud account. Templates include route names, geometry and opaque workout association identifiers, but no workout timestamps or actual performance totals. Progress, matching results and charts are calculated locally from authorized HealthKit history. Anonymous analytics events are stored locally on-device only in a ring-buffer log.

3. Data We Don't Collect

We do not operate an advertising profile, sell personal or health data, or share it with data brokers or advertising networks. We do not receive your HealthKit database, payment-card information, or on-device analytics log.

4. HealthKit

RunCalc reads workouts and routes solely to provide app features and does not upload your HealthKit database to a RunCalc-operated server. Its app database is excluded from automatic device backups. RunCalc never writes to HealthKit.

5. Analytics

Anonymous analytics events (such as paywall views and subscription events) are stored in a ring-buffer log on your device only. They are never transmitted to any external service.

6. Retention, Deletion and Access Choices

RunCalc retains synced goal and route definitions until you delete them. Deletions propagate to your other devices when sync completes; deletion markers remain to prevent old offline copies from restoring deleted records. Removing the app deletes local analysis but does not delete synced definitions. Delete a route by swiping it in Routes; Delete All Routes removes the saved route library. Delete goals in Goals. These controls may require Pro where the relevant feature is gated. To remove all local app data without a subscription, use iOS Settings → General → iPhone Storage (or iPad Storage) → RunCalc → Delete App. Offload App retains its data and is not deletion. Deleting RunCalc does not delete your original Apple Health workouts.

Withdraw Health access using Apple Health → profile → Apps and Services → RunCalc and turn off its permissions. iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health may also provide these controls. Revoking access prevents future reads but does not delete saved copies. Location access can be changed in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → RunCalc.

Earlier TestFlight builds used your private iCloud database for saved routes, GPS, goals and related records. The revised release copies downloaded definitions into a separate definitions-only sync store and keeps workout-derived caches local. It does not erase the older private-cloud records. New installations recover synced definitions after signing in to the same Apple account; workout history still requires HealthKit access. If RunCalc is listed under iOS Settings → your Apple Account → iCloud → Storage/Manage Account Storage, its data-deletion control may remove all RunCalc cloud data, including the new synced definitions; it is not a selective legacy-cache cleanup tool. Export and verify your retained data first. Availability and labels vary by OS. If no control appears, contact support for guidance rather than assuming uninstall removed cloud data. Update all devices before removing old cloud data.

Before deleting anything, Settings → Your Data → Export Routes and Goals can save a JSON copy. Exports contain precise location information; choose a private destination. Copies you export, including to iCloud Drive, remain wherever you save them until you remove them there. The export is not an in-app restore feature. Contact support to request help; we do not have access to your local Health data or private iCloud database.

7. Subscriptions

Subscription transactions are handled entirely by Apple's StoreKit framework. RunCalc does not process or store any payment information.

8. Service Providers

RunCalc contains no third-party advertising, tracking, or analytics SDKs. Apple provides private CloudKit storage, HealthKit and StoreKit as operating-system services used by the app.

9. Children

RunCalc is not directed at children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
