Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

1. Introduction

This policy explains what data RunCalc accesses, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

2. Health and workout data

With your permission, RunCalc reads workouts, workout routes, heart rate, active energy, and related fitness information from Apple Health to provide its features. This information is processed on your device and is not transmitted to Flewsoft servers.

3. Data stored by RunCalc

RunCalc stores app preferences, goals, saved routes, matching results, and a limited on-device activity log needed to operate and improve the app. This information remains in the app’s local storage unless a future feature clearly tells you otherwise and asks for permission.

4. Analytics and advertising

RunCalc does not include third-party advertising, tracking, or analytics SDKs. Limited diagnostic and subscription events are recorded locally on your device and are not sent to an external analytics service.

5. Purchases

Apple processes RunCalc Pro purchases and subscription transactions through the App Store. RunCalc does not receive or store your payment-card details.

6. Sharing and selling data

Flewsoft does not sell your personal information or health data. RunCalc does not share HealthKit data with advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties.

7. Your choices

You can review or revoke RunCalc’s Apple Health permissions in iOS Settings. You can remove locally stored RunCalc data by deleting it within the app where available or by deleting the app.

8. Children

RunCalc is not directed to children under 13, and Flewsoft does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as RunCalc changes. The revision date above identifies the current version.

10. Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to support@runcalc.app.
