Frequently Asked Questions

What happens to my data if I cancel?

Saved routes and goals are preserved. Pro features, including access to the Routes tab, require an active subscription. You can export saved routes and goal definitions from Settings without Pro.

Is there a family plan?

Not currently. RunCalc Pro covers one Apple ID.

Can I try before buying?

Eligible subscribers may receive a 14-day free trial. The App Store confirms eligibility, the trial period, and the price before purchase.
