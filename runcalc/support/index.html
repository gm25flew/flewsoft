RunCalc Support

Answers to common questions about workouts, routes, privacy, and subscriptions.

How do I grant Apple Health access?

Open iOS Settings, choose Privacy & Security, then Health and RunCalc. Enable the workout information you want RunCalc to read.

Why aren’t my workouts showing?

Confirm that Apple Health access is enabled. RunCalc loads recent workouts first and may continue processing older workouts in the background.

How does route matching work?

RunCalc compares GPS points from your Apple Health workouts with your saved routes. A workout is linked when its path is sufficiently similar to the route.

How do I restore RunCalc Pro?

Open RunCalc Settings and choose Restore Purchases while signed into the Apple Account that originally purchased the subscription.

How do I manage or cancel a subscription?

Open iOS Settings, tap your name, then choose Subscriptions and RunCalc. Apple handles billing, renewals, and cancellation.

What happens if my subscription ends?

Your workouts remain in Apple Health, and RunCalc preserves its locally stored goals and routes. Pro editing and analysis features become unavailable until the subscription is renewed.

Does RunCalc send my health data anywhere?

No. Workout and route information is processed on your device and is not transmitted to Flewsoft servers.

Can I use RunCalc offline?

Previously loaded workouts and core analysis work without a network connection. App Store purchases, subscription updates, and some Apple services require connectivity.

Still need help?

Email support@runcalc.app and include your iPhone model, iOS version, and a short description of the problem. Please do not send health records or other sensitive information.
