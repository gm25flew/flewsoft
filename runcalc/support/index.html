Support

Answers to the most common questions about RunCalc.

How do I grant HealthKit access?

Open iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → RunCalc, and enable all permissions.

Why aren't my workouts showing?

Make sure HealthKit permissions are granted (see above). The app fetches recent workouts first; older workouts load in the background.

How does route matching work?

RunCalc uses GPS data from your workouts to compare against saved routes using a similarity algorithm. Workouts that closely match a route are automatically linked.

What is RunCalc Pro?

RunCalc Pro unlocks the full workout history, all interactive charts, route management, goal tracking, smart suggestions, and athlete stats. Eligible subscribers may receive a 14-day free trial.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to iOS Settings → your Apple ID → Subscriptions → RunCalc. You can cancel or manage your subscription there.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

Apple Health retains your original workouts. RunCalc preserves its local saved routes, goals, and analysis. Pro features, including access to Routes, require an active subscription. Export Routes and Goals in Settings remains available without Pro.

Is my HealthKit data sent anywhere?

The revised 1.0 release syncs goal definitions and reusable route templates through private iCloud. It rebuilds progress, matches and charts locally from your authorized HealthKit history. Earlier TestFlight builds used private iCloud sync; see our privacy policy for details and removal guidance.

Can I use RunCalc offline?

Yes. Saved routes, goals and workout analysis are local. An internet connection is needed for App Store purchases and subscription verification; some Apple Health data may need to finish downloading.
